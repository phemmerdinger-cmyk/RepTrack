RepTrack Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026

RepTrack is a workout logging website with an optional connection to WHOOP. This page explains how the current version handles data. The service is intended for personal fitness tracking and is not a medical service.

What data the app handles

Workouts you enter, including exercise names, sets, weights, repetitions, and dates, are stored in the local storage of the browser where you use RepTrack. RepTrack does not currently have an account database or sync your workout logs between devices.

If you choose to connect WHOOP, RepTrack requests your permission to read recovery, sleep, and cycle information, which can include recovery score, HRV, resting heart rate, and strain data. RepTrack retrieves this information from WHOOP when you request it and displays it on the page.

WHOOP access and authentication

WHOOP authentication is handled using OAuth. You sign in through WHOOP and can choose whether to grant access. RepTrack stores WHOOP access credentials in an encrypted, HttpOnly cookie on your device to maintain the connection. The website server processes these credentials when requesting data from WHOOP. RepTrack is not designed to write a permanent database of WHOOP readings. Hosting infrastructure may process technical request information as part of serving the site.

How data is used and shared

Information is used to show your personal workout history and any WHOOP fitness metrics you authorize. The current application does not sell fitness data or use it for advertising. WHOOP data is obtained from WHOOP under the permissions you grant; WHOOP separately controls information in your WHOOP account under its own privacy policy. The site is hosted through Render.

Retention, deletion, and choices

You can delete locally logged workouts within RepTrack or clear your browser's storage for this site. You may also export a backup file if you choose. To end WHOOP access in RepTrack, use its Disconnect feature, which clears the app's authentication cookie in that browser. You can also revoke the application's access from your WHOOP account. Server or hosting-provider technical logs may be retained according to provider policies.

Security

The hosted website uses HTTPS. The WHOOP authentication cookie is encrypted and configured as Secure, HttpOnly, and SameSite=Lax. No online service can guarantee perfect security. Do not use RepTrack to store confidential medical information.

Contact

For questions about RepTrack privacy, use the contact email listed for the RepTrack application in the WHOOP Developer Dashboard. Do not post private health information in a public GitHub issue.

Changes

This policy may change if RepTrack adds accounts, cloud syncing, analytics, or other features. The latest version will be posted at this URL.

RepTrack is an independent application and is not operated by WHOOP.
