DAILY PERCENTAGE ROTATION

The daily percentages rotate intentionally: Heavy (75%+), Moderate (55–74%), Light (40–54%), and Recovery (below 40%). Actual weights and reps remain unchanged.

DAILY BACK SQUAT · 30 DAYS

Your saved warmups and two working-set targets appear on every date. Only logged sets are completed.

How this plan works

The 30-day schedule alternates heavy, moderate, light, and recovery-intensity squat days to vary load. You still have two working sets each day, preceded by your listed warmups. This is a plan, not a requirement to lift through pain or significant fatigue. Only a saved Back Squat set completes a day.
